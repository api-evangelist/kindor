Kindor already supported automated operational reports, helping engineering organizations receive recurring sprint and monthly summaries directly via email. However, organizations could not yet configure report schedules, generate reports on demand, or browse previously generated reports. Today, we’re introducing a new Reports Management experience in Kindor that brings these capabilities into the product through a centralized self-service interface.
